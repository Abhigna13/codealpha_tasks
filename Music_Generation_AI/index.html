🎵 MusicGenerationAI

An AI-powered Music Generation Platform developed using Python, Streamlit, Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch, and MusicGen.

Project Features

  • AI-Based Music Generation
  • Text-to-Music Generation
  • MusicGen AI Model
  • Custom Music Prompts
  • Genre and Mood Selection
  • Creativity Control
  • Generated Music Library
  • Favorites and Playlist Management
  • Listening Statistics
  • Music Generation History and Activity Tracking

Technologies Used

Python | Streamlit | MusicGen | Hugging Face Transformers | PyTorch | SciPy
